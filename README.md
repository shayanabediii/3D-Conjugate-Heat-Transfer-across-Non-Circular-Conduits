# 3D Conjugate Heat Transfer & Crossflow Aerodynamics across Non-Circular Conduits

## Overview
Comparative 3D numerical analysis evaluating thermal-hydraulic performance characteristics of circular, square, and hexagonal conduits and microchannel cooling passages.

## Numerical Details
- **Solver Formulation:** ANSYS Fluent Coupled pressure-based solver evaluating solid-fluid conjugate heat transfer (CHT).
- **Meshing Technique:** 3D boundary layer inflation grids resolving local temperature gradients and Nusselt number ($Nu$) distributions.

## Findings & Conclusions
- Square conduits yielded **$7\%$ to $14\%$ higher total heat transfer rates** compared to standard circular tubes.
- Associated hydraulic pumping penalty was limited to only **$0.5\%$**.
- Numerically validated against empirical heat transfer correlations within **$1.3\%$ to $6.8\%$** error bounds.

## Tools Used
- ANSYS Fluent, CFD-Post
